Renewable energy

Solar and wind power generate electricity from naturally replenished sources. Storage and reliable grids help match supply with demand.

♻️

Reduce before recycling

Avoiding unnecessary items and reusing what we already have usually prevents more waste than recycling alone.

🌳

Protect biodiversity

Healthy ecosystems support food, clean water, pollination, and climate resilience. Protecting habitats helps many species at once.

🚲

Everyday eco choices

Walking, cycling, saving energy, repairing items, and wasting less food can reduce environmental impact when practical and accessible.
