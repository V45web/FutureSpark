THE BIG PICTURE

Ideas become impact

Innovation is not only about futuristic machines. It can be a better way to save water, make learning easier, or solve a local problem. Start with a need, test an idea, learn from feedback, and improve it.

Test what you know

Choose a quick interactive activity.

💡

Start with a real need

Observe a problem, talk to the people affected, and define what a better outcome would look like before choosing a solution.

🧩

Prototype and test

A sketch, cardboard model, or simple demo can reveal what works. Early tests make it easier to learn and improve without wasting resources.

🔁

Learn from feedback

Useful feedback is specific. Ask what was clear, what was difficult, and what one change would make the idea better.

🌱

Innovation can be simple

A refill station, a clearer school timetable, or a way to reuse materials can be as meaningful as a high-tech invention.
